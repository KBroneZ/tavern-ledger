Game log setup

    How the overlay works

    Also in the tray menu. It shows over Hearthstone only while a game is on; hover a hero on the game's leaderboard to see the last board you met. "Arrange overlay" lets you move the panels and fit the leaderboard box (the whole screen takes clicks until you press "Lock overlay"). Exclusive fullscreen hides any overlay: run Hearthstone windowed or borderless.

    Upload to the website…

    The link opens in your browser and signs this app in; the app never asks for your password. Accounts are created on the website.

    Signed in as

    Off until you turn it on. Each finished game's report goes up: heroes, card ids, places and boards. Never player names, BattleTags or ratings.

      Shop and actions

      About this

      Averages per game over the games whose shop the log has from start to end. Only your own shop is in the log.

      Hero picks

      About this

      The heroes the game offered you at the start of each game, and the one you picked. Only your own offers are in the log.

      Trinkets and Dark Gifts

      About this

      How often each one was offered to you, how often you took it, and your average place when you did. Only your own choices are in the log.

      Curious stats

      About these totals

      Totals over all your games, Solo and Duos together. Each one says where it comes from and how many games back it; a game saved before a value was kept is left out of that count, never added as zero.

      Where values come from

      A value with no mark comes straight from the game's log. Every other value carries a mark, and hovering any value says where it comes from.
