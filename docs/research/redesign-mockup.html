Lamplight glass: the screen splits into panes as you scroll

From your notes: the scene stays still and, seen from the side, the game screen separates into panes of glass, one layer at a time; then the page carries on. A tavern at night without borrowing the game's own colours: smoky warm black, amber lamplight, frosted glass with a thin prism edge. No frames around minions anywhere.

Mockup only. Every number, hero name and minion name is made up, and the game is drawn with our own simple shapes, no game art. Fonts (Bricolage Grotesque and Figtree, SIL OFL 1.1) load from Google Fonts on this page only; the app and the site would ship the files. The scroll scene needs a current Chrome, Edge or Safari; elsewhere, or when you ask for less motion, it shows the panes already apart, with every caption.

Safe for your account

  • It never shows what you could not see on your own screen.
