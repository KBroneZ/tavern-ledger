# Logo prompts

Ready-to-paste prompts for the directions in [logo-brief.md](logo-brief.md). Paste them yourself into ChatGPT (image generation) or the other tools; the results are references only, to be redrawn as a hand-made SVG later (see the brief, "After the user picks one").

Tips: one direction per chat; ask for a single image per message; if a result adds text, gradients or game-like details, use the follow-up prompts at the end.

## Shared "do not" list

Every prompt below already includes it. Keep it if you write your own:

> Do not include: any Blizzard, Hearthstone or Battlegrounds logo, name, card frame, mana gem, coin, tavern sign, swirl, hero portrait or game typeface; anything that looks like an official game asset; gradients, 3D, bevels, textures, drop shadows or photo realism; fine detail or thin lines that disappear at 16 px; text inside the mark-only version; extra words, taglines or fake letters.

## 1. Ledger and flame (ChatGPT)

```text
Design a logo for "Tavern Ledger", a free desktop app and website that tracks your games in a fantasy card game, with the feeling of a ledger read by lamplight in a fantasy tavern, drawn in a modern, minimal way.

Concept: an open book seen from the front, its two pages forming a shallow V, with a single lamp flame rising from the spine.

Style: flat vector logo, 2 to 4 simple solid shapes, bold geometry, thick strokes, no outlines thinner than 1/16 of the mark's width, must stay readable as a 16 px favicon. Centred on a plain flat background with generous margin.

Colours (flat, no gradients): background #120d0b (smoky warm black), mark in amber #ffb24a with highlights in #ffd08a, any dark detail in #2a1600, text in warm white #f7efe6.

Show two versions side by side on the same background: (1) the mark alone, no text; (2) the mark on the left with the wordmark "Tavern Ledger" to its right, horizontal, in a clean modern grotesque sans-serif, bold, spelled exactly "Tavern Ledger".

Do not include: any Blizzard, Hearthstone or Battlegrounds logo, name, card frame, mana gem, coin, tavern sign, swirl, hero portrait or game typeface; anything that looks like an official game asset; gradients, 3D, bevels, textures, drop shadows or photo realism; fine detail or thin lines that disappear at 16 px; text inside the mark-only version; extra words, taglines or fake letters.
```

## Variants for vector tools

Recraft and Ideogram can make logos; Recraft can export SVG. Their terms on ownership and on public galleries are **not verified** (see the brief): read them first. These tools work better with shorter prompts and a separate negative prompt where they have one.

### Recraft (style: "Vector art" or "Icon", export SVG)

```text
Minimal flat vector logo mark for "Tavern Ledger", an app that tracks games: [CONCEPT]. 2 to 4 bold solid shapes, thick strokes, readable at 16 px, centred on a plain #120d0b background, mark in #ffb24a with #ffd08a highlights, dark details #2a1600. No text, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no outlines thinner than the mark's 1/16.
```

Replace `[CONCEPT]` with one line, for example "an open book whose two pages form a shallow V, with a single lamp flame rising from the spine". Add the colours with Recraft's palette picker too, if it offers one. Make the wordmark separately in our own font later; do not ask a vector tool for it.

### Ideogram (good with lettering; use for the mark + wordmark version)

```text
Flat vector logo, horizontal lockup: on the left [CONCEPT], on the right the words "Tavern Ledger" in a bold modern grotesque sans-serif. Smoky warm black background #120d0b, amber #ffb24a mark, warm white #f7efe6 lettering. Minimal, 2 to 4 bold shapes, readable at small sizes, centred, generous margin.
```

Negative prompt (where the tool has a field for it):

```text
Blizzard, Hearthstone, Battlegrounds, game logo, card frame, gem, coin, tavern sign, swirl, hero portrait, gradient, 3D, bevel, texture, drop shadow, photo, thin lines, tagline, extra text
```

## Follow-up prompts (any tool)

Paste one at a time after a result you like:

```text
Simplify the mark to three shapes at most, keep the same idea and colours, flat, no gradient.
```

```text
Make a single-colour version: the whole mark in #ffb24a on #120d0b, and the same mark in #2a1600 on #f7efe6 next to it.
```

```text
Show the mark-only version at real sizes on #120d0b: 256 px, 64 px, 32 px and 16 px, in a row, without smoothing it or adding detail at the small sizes.
```

```text
Thicken every stroke and remove any detail that is not visible at 32 px. Keep the silhouette.
```

```text
Show the mark-only version inside a circle crop and inside a rounded square, as an app icon and a profile picture.
```

```text
Make it look less like a game asset and more like a modern software tool: fewer ornaments, simpler geometry, same idea.
```
