Tavern Ledger · logo concepts · T-521

Twelve marks, drawn by hand

Each concept is a small hand-made SVG on a 64 × 64 grid: a few flat shapes, no text, no gradients, in the site's amber on smoky black. Every one is shown at 256, 64, 32 and 16 px, on dark, on light and in one colour, plus a true 16-pixel render blown up four times so you see what a favicon or the taskbar really gets.

Answer with numbers, for example "2 and 8", and say what to keep or change.

All twelve

01

Diamond tile

Dark
Light
One colour
16 px, real pixels ×4

Idea. A T and an L cut from one square and turned 45°: the L becomes the cradle at the bottom, the T leans in from the top right. The silhouette is a hanging-sign diamond, the letters are the pieces.

Weak point. The letters only appear once you are told; at 16 px it reads as a diamond with two slots.

02

Rising diamond

Dark
Light
One colour
16 px, real pixels ×4

Idea. A diamond split in two: a pointed T (an arrow climbing) rises out of a cup that is an L turned 45°. Climbing the ladder, held in the ledger.

Weak point. The arrow can read as a generic "upload" or "level up" sign.

03

Shared stem

Dark
Light
One colour
16 px, real pixels ×4

Idea. T and L share one stem: the bar of the T on top, the foot of the L at the bottom. One stroke family, no container.

Weak point. Plain; a stranger may read a stray "F" turned over, or nothing at all.

04

Lantern book

Dark
Light
One colour
16 px, real pixels ×4

Idea. A lantern whose glass is an open book: the cap and the base are the lantern, the glowing pages are the light. No frame, no ring.

Weak point. Without the frame the cap and base can read as a hat and a shelf; it needs the two colours to say "glow".

05

Pen and sword

Dark
Light
One colour
16 px, real pixels ×4

Idea. A quill crossed over a sword: the ledger and the fight. The quill sits on top, cut clean into the blade.

Weak point. Crossed objects are a heraldic cliché and the busiest mark here; at 16 px it is an X with a lump, and the quill can pass for a leaf.

06

Flame chart

Dark
Light
One colour
16 px, real pixels ×4

Idea. A flame cut into three bars that rise to the right: lamplight and your numbers going up, in one silhouette.

Weak point. Close to a generic "trending" or "hot" icon; the flame tip must stay sharp or it is just a bar chart.

07

Dog-ear

Dark
Light
One colour
16 px, real pixels ×4

Idea. A ledger page with its corner folded down, and a rising line cut through it that ends at the fold: the page you turn when you climb.

Weak point. The page-with-a-fold is every "document" icon; the cut line is what makes it ours, and it is thin at 16 px.

08

Candle bookmark

Dark
Light
One colour
16 px, real pixels ×4

Idea. A bookmark ribbon that is also a candle: the notch says "your place in the ledger", the flame says lamplight.

Weak point. Could read as a plain candle or a banner; the notch is the only ledger cue.

09

Stair flame

Dark
Light
One colour
16 px, real pixels ×4

Idea. Three steps up to a flame on the top step: the climb, with the light waiting at the top.

Weak point. Stairs are also an "exit" or "stairwell" sign; the flame has to stay big.

10

Bar tankard

Dark
Light
One colour
16 px, real pixels ×4

Idea. A tankard whose head is three bars rising to the right: the tavern below, your numbers on top.

Weak point. Can pass for a coffee, beer or "stats" icon; the bars and the handle are what keep it from being a plain mug.

11

Quill

Dark
Light
One colour
16 px, real pixels ×4

Idea. One bold quill with a flame-like lick cut into its vane: the ledger, written by hand by lamplight.

Weak point. Quills are common for writing apps; at 16 px the slit closes and the flame lick is gone, so it is just a feather.

12

Tally gate

Dark
Light
One colour
16 px, real pixels ×4

Idea. Four strokes and a slash: the oldest ledger there is, counting games on the tavern wall.

Weak point. Generic: tally marks belong to everyone, and the four strokes nearly merge at 16 px.
